# The 10-Factor Hire

**An open standard for hiring that holds both sides accountable.**

Hiring is broken in both directions. Candidates lie on resumes. Employers ghost, misrepresent roles, and hide behind anonymous job posts. The 10-Factor Hire is an opinionated, vendor-neutral standard that defines what a trustworthy hire looks like — for the person applying *and* the company hiring.

The same 10 factors apply to both sides. No exceptions.

---

## The 10 factors

1. **Verified Identity** — Real human, real ID, on both sides.
2. **Intro Video** — Both sides show themselves before the interview.
3. **Proof of Skill** — Show the work, on camera, with the person visible.
4. **Work Over Timeline** — Capability beats chronology.
5. **Deal-Breakers Up Front** — Schedule, location, pay, start date — surfaced first.
6. **Fewer, Better, Faster** — Quality over volume on both sides.
7. **Shareability** — Profiles and job posts are portable; the user owns the record.
8. **No Ghosting** — Every applicant gets a response. Every interview gets a decision.
9. **Outcomes Tracked** — Did the hire stick? Both sides report back.
10. **No Homework** — Skills are proven without unpaid take-homes.

Each factor has a full doctrine page in [`/factors`](./factors).

---

## What this standard is

- **Bidirectional.** Every factor binds both candidates and employers.
- **Vendor-neutral.** Platforms, ATSs, recruiting agencies, and in-house teams can all adopt it.
- **Falsifiable.** Each factor states a clear behavior. Either you meet it or you don't.
- **Open.** Licensed CC BY 4.0. Fork it, adapt it, build on it.

## What this standard is not

- Not a certification. There is no badge to buy.
- Not a product. It is doctrine. Any implementation is separate.
- Not a checklist. It is a stance on what hiring should look like.
